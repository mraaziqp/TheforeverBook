# Forever Book Template Usage Guide

## 📚 How to Create Books from Templates

### **Step 1: Sign In**
1. Open the Forever Book app
2. Click **"Sign In"** or **"Google Auth"**
3. Use your email and password (or Google account)

### **Step 2: Choose Template**
You have **two ways** to start with a template:

#### **Method A: Featured Templates (Easiest)**
1. On home page, see "Featured Book Styles"
2. Choose one:
   - **The Minimalist** - Clean, simple layouts
   - **Cinematic Editorial** - Bold, full-bleed designs
   - **Classic Vintage** - Warm, nostalgic feeling
3. Click **"Use Template"** button
4. Enter your book name
5. ✅ Book created with template pre-applied!

#### **Method B: Template Library (More Options)**
1. Go to **Studio Canvas** tab
2. In left sidebar, see **Template Library**
3. Browse categories:
   - Engagement Special 💖
   - Cinematic
   - Editorial
   - Minimalist
   - Brutalist
   - Custom Styles ✨
4. Click any template to apply it
5. Start editing on canvas

### **Step 3: Customize Template**
1. Use the canvas editor to:
   - Drag images onto placeholders
   - Edit text
   - Adjust colors and styling
   - Add/remove elements
2. Changes auto-save to Firebase

### **Step 4: Save Custom Templates**
Love a layout? Save it!
1. Design your spread
2. In Template Library, click **"Save Current Spread"**
3. Name your custom style
4. ✅ Saved under "Custom Styles" category
5. Reuse anytime in this project!

### **Step 5: Export Your Book**
1. Click **"Print"** button (top right)
2. Choose format:
   - PDF (for digital sharing)
   - Image (for social media)
3. Adjust quality/resolution
4. Download! 📥

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I use different templates for different pages?**  
A: Yes! Each page can have its own template. Just apply a new template when you navigate to a different page.

**Q: Can I edit template layouts?**  
A: Yes! Templates are starting points. Click, drag, and customize anything on the canvas.

**Q: Can I create completely custom templates?**  
A: Yes! Design your perfect spread, then click "Save Current Spread" to save as a custom template.

**Q: Do my templates sync across devices?**  
A: Yes! Custom templates are saved to your Firebase account, so they're available on any device.

**Q: Can I share templates with others?**  
A: Not yet, but this is planned! For now, templates are personal to your account.

**Q: What if I don't like the template?**  
A: Start fresh! Go back to "Home" and create a new book with a different template, or manually clear the current page.
